## Full description

Play limit: 4,000 characters. This is about 2,150.

```
Maruti Water is the dealer application for Maruti Water Solution. It gives
approved wholesalers and retailers the current product catalogue, their own
pricing, and the tools to quote a customer, check a warranty and raise a
service request from the shop floor.

Access is for registered dealers. Create an account with your firm details and
GST number, and Maruti Water Solution reviews it before the account is
activated.

PRICING THAT MATCHES YOUR ACCOUNT
Wholesalers see wholesale pricing and retailers see retail pricing, alongside
the MRP and the dealer margin. No shared price list, no separate PDF to keep
up to date.

THE FULL CATALOGUE
Browse every domestic, commercial and industrial model, plus spare parts and
accessories. Search by name, model number or product code, filter by category
and availability, and sort by price. Each product page carries the
specifications, images and warranty period.

QUOTE WITHOUT PAPERWORK
Save the products a customer is considering, then export the list as a PDF
quotation and send it on WhatsApp or email.

SCAN A PRODUCT, SEE ITS HISTORY
Point the camera at the QR code or barcode on a Maruti Water Solution label,
or type the code in. The app opens the product, or the individual unit if the
label is a serial number.

WARRANTY AT A GLANCE
For any unit you scan, the app shows whether the warranty is active or
expired, the coverage period, the installation date, the expiry date, and the
registered customer name, phone number and invoice number.

SERVICE AND SUPPORT
Raise a complaint against a product or a unit, choose the category and
priority, attach photos, and follow it by ticket number until it is resolved.

FOR MARUTI WATER SOLUTION STAFF
Owner accounts add product and price management, approval of new dealer
registrations, printable QR label sheets, promotional banners and warranty
claim review.

IN ENGLISH AND GUJARATI
The whole app, including product details and warranty information, is
available in both languages.

Maruti Water Solution, Botad, Gujarat.
```

## Still needed before submission

**Photo and Video Permissions declaration:** does not apply. The manifest
declares `CAMERA` and `INTERNET` only — verified with `aapt2 dump permissions`
on the release artifact, and with `dumpsys package` on an installed build.
`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` and `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` were removed because nothing
used them: every image enters through `ImagePicker().pickImage`, which hands
the choice to the system picker and returns a single content URI. `main.dart`
now sets `useAndroidPhotoPicker = true`, so Android 13-15 gets the Photo
Picker rather than the old document browser. If Play still shows the
declaration, the bundle being reviewed is an older one.

**App access:** this app cannot be used without signing in, and sign-in needs
an account Maruti Water Solution has approved. Play's review team will reject
the listing unless working credentials are supplied under
Policy > App content > App access. Create a reviewer account rather than
handing over the owner login.
